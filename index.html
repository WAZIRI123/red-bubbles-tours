The Red Bubbles way

Born on the island, we know exactly where the tide will take you.

Red Bubbles Tours designs private journeys across Zanzibar, Tanzania and East Africa. We don't sell packages off a shelf — every trip is built around you, placed with partners we trust, and looked after by our own team from the moment you land.

Slow days on white-sand beaches, sunrise game drives on the Serengeti, spice, culture and Swahili hospitality — everything is taken care of, so all you have to do is arrive.
